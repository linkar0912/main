## Why people look for a Manychat alternative in India

Manychat's own pages explain the main differences an Indian buyer will notice:

- **Currency and tax.** Manychat's public prices are in US dollars, "Prices do not include tax", and the price depends on your billing country.
- **The meter.** Manychat counts Active Contacts, the people you exchange messages with each month. Its Free plan includes 25. Past your plan's allowance, each extra person is charged unless you set an overage limit.
- **A newer pricing model.** Manychat's help centre says its current plans began on 2 March 2026 and that older accounts are moving to them country by country.

None of this makes Manychat a poor choice. It connects TikTok, Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS and email as well as Instagram and Messenger, includes AI on Pro and above, and offers a 14-day trial of Essential or Pro. The tools above suit people who want rupee pricing or a different way of counting usage.

## Before you switch

1. Write down every live Manychat automation: the post, keyword, message and link.
2. Check which plan of the new tool includes each step you use. Follow gates, email capture and Story replies are on different plans in different tools.
3. Rebuild your busiest automation first and test it from a second account.
4. Turn off the old automation before turning on the new one, so nobody gets two replies.
5. Watch the first day of real conversations before moving the rest.
