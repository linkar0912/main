# Linkar vs Manychat for Instagram automation in India

Both tools turn Instagram comments and messages into automatic replies. They are built for different buyers. Here is how they compare on price, limits and features, using each company's own public pages.

**Linkar is our product.** We checked every Manychat fact below on Manychat's own website on 15 October 2026 and link to the page it came from. If anything has changed, tell us at the support address and we will correct it.

## The short answer

Choose **Linkar** if you are a creator or small business in India who wants Instagram and Facebook Page replies, prices in rupees with GST included, UPI payment, and a monthly limit that stops instead of adding charges.

Choose **Manychat** if you need more channels than Instagram and Facebook Pages (Manychat lists TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS and email), AI-written replies, a paid-plan free trial, or integrations with a wider set of marketing tools.

### The two meters work differently

Linkar counts **messages it sends**. A comment reply, a DM, a sequence step and a broadcast message each count once. Messages people send you cost nothing. At the limit, Linkar stops sending and tells you, and nothing extra is billed.

Manychat counts **people**. Every person you exchange messages with in a month is one Active Contact, however many messages you trade. Past the included number, Manychat charges for each extra contact unless you set a limit on overages.

Neither meter is better for everyone. If you send many messages to few people, counting people can work out well. If many different people each get one or two messages, counting messages usually does.

## Where Manychat is the better fit

- **You need more channels.** Manychat connects TikTok, Telegram, WhatsApp, SMS and email as well as Instagram and Messenger. Linkar covers Instagram and Facebook Page comments only.
- **You want AI to write replies.** Manychat AI answers FAQs and guides conversations on Pro and above. Linkar sends the replies you write and hands anything else to your team.
- **You want Facebook Messenger chats.** Linkar's Facebook automations post public replies under Page comments. They do not send Messenger messages.
- **You want to try a paid plan free.** Manychat offers 14 days on Essential or Pro and a refund within 30 days of the first purchase. Linkar has no paid trial; the Free plan is how you try it, and a billing period that has started is not refunded.
- **You want a DM when someone follows.** Manychat advertises this. Linkar starts only from something the person does, such as a comment, a Story reply or a message.

## Where Linkar is the better fit

- **You pay in rupees.** Prices are in INR with GST included, and you can pay by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay.
- **You want a bill that cannot grow on its own.** When you reach the monthly limit, Linkar stops sending rather than charging for more.
- **Many different people message you.** Linkar counts messages, not people, so a busy comment thread on the Free or Creator plan does not create per-person charges.
- **You run a Facebook Page as well.** Public comment replies on a Facebook Page are included on every plan, Free included.
- **You want a small team on a small plan.** Creator includes two seats for ₹199 a month.

## Moving from Manychat to Linkar

Linkar does not import Manychat automations. You rebuild each one, which is also a good moment to tidy them.

1. List the Manychat automations that are live, with their keywords, posts and links.
2. Create a free Linkar account and connect the same Instagram account and, if you use one, your Facebook Page.
3. Rebuild your busiest automation first. Keep the same keyword so existing posts still work.
4. Test from a second account: the expected keyword, a longer sentence, and an unrelated comment.
5. Turn off the matching Manychat automation before you turn on Linkar's, so people do not get two replies.
6. Check the first real conversations in Linkar's activity view, then move the next automation.

## Questions

**Is Linkar cheaper than Manychat?**
It depends on how you use it. Linkar's paid plans start at ₹199 a month with GST included and count the messages Linkar sends. Manychat's start at $17 a month on monthly billing, before tax, and count the people you talk to. The worked examples above show both meters on the same month.

**Does Manychat have a free plan in India?**
Manychat's pricing page shows a Free plan with 25 Active Contacts a month and up to 4 live triggers. It says prices depend on your billing country, so check the page from your own account.

**Can Linkar reply with AI?**
No. Linkar sends the replies you write, matched by keyword, post or trigger. Questions that need a personal answer can be handed to your team.

**Does Linkar work with WhatsApp?**
No. Linkar works with Instagram and Facebook Pages. Manychat connects WhatsApp on its Pro plan and above.

**Is Linkar safe for my Instagram account?**
Linkar connects through Meta's official Instagram API and never asks for your Instagram password. [OWNER CHECK: confirm the wording against the current Meta App Review status before publishing.]

**Can I use Linkar and Manychat together?**
You can, but do not point both at the same post or keyword, or people will get two replies.
