# Linkar SEO audit, 15 October 2026

**Goal:** become the strongest result for "Instagram automation" searches, especially in India.
**Business type:** SaaS (pricing page, sign-up, feature catalogue, INR plans).

**Two targets.** Every finding below says which one it concerns:

- **LIVE**: https://linkar.in as deployed today. That is the homepage, `/pricing`, the legal and help pages, `/login` and `/signup`.
- **LOCAL**: the repo build at http://localhost:3000. This is `main` at `220f238`, 12 commits ahead of `origin/main`, plus uncommitted work: the SEO catalogue and the first round of fixes (G1 to G6, section 11). It adds the `/features`, `/solutions` and `/templates` hubs, 64 detail pages, `/llms.txt` and the recipe downloads. None of it is deployed.

**How the score was made:** the weighted method in the `seo` skill. Inputs:

- **17 specialist reports.** The first pass returned 5: seo-page, seo-programmatic, seo-images, seo-hreflang and seo-competitor-pages. The other 12 were re-run later on 15 Oct, after G1 to G6 had been applied, so their LOCAL results describe the current build: seo-technical, seo-content, seo-schema, seo-sitemap, seo-performance, seo-visual, seo-geo, seo-google, seo-backlinks, seo-sxo, seo-cluster and seo-drift.
- **My own checks:** curl of both targets, the production build in `.next`, reading the repo, and read-only Search Console, GA4, PageSpeed, Bing, Common Crawl and Wayback Machine data. Every new or conflicting claim from the re-run was checked again before it went in (section 4).

The full specialist reports and the key raw data are copied into [2026-10-15/](2026-10-15/).

Linkar gets zero search impressions today because Google has not indexed a single URL.

- Google crawled the homepage over HTTPS on 24 September and chose not to index it. That crawl predates the canonical tag, robots.txt and sitemap, which shipped on 10 October.
- On 15 October at 07:37 UTC Googlebot crawled `http://linkar.in/` and picked the **HTTP** URL as its own canonical, ignoring the declared HTTPS one. HTTP still returns 200 on every host.
- Google does not know `/pricing` exists.
- No site links to linkar.in. Bing records 0 links, and the domain is absent from the newest Common Crawl web graph (July to September 2026).
- The name is ambiguous. Searches return a French vehicle-warranty "Linkar", a UK social rewards app and DM Linkr, an Instagram comment-to-DM tool. Google's AI Overview defines "Linkar" as the UK app.
- The domain has a history: parked ad redirects from 2022 to 2025, and two unrelated blogs before that.
- The page Google crawled does not say "Instagram automation" anywhere.

The local build fixes most of the on-page and technical work (G1 to G6): keyword titles and H1s on all 79 pages, linked schema, four sitemaps with image entries, a full llms.txt, analytics coverage and no root loading skeleton. None of it helps until it is deployed and indexed. The local build also has three problems of its own:

- **Thin catalogue text.** On average 38.2% of a page's text is unique to it. 45 of 64 pages are under 40%, and 2 are under the 30% hard stop.
- **A CSS regression in the production build.** Render-blocking CSS is 43% larger on `/` and 66% larger on `/pricing` than on live, because Turbopack's default chunking merges catalogue CSS into chunks those pages load.
- **Two pages chase the head term.** The `/solutions` hub competes with the homepage for "Instagram automation".

**Top 5 issues**

1. **Nothing is indexed, and Google prefers the HTTP homepage (Critical and High, LIVE).** The homepage is "Crawled, currently not indexed", `/pricing` is unknown to Google, and Search Console shows 0 impressions in 480 days ([C1](#critical), [H8](#high)).
2. **Catalogue uniqueness is below the quality gates (Critical and High, LOCAL).** G5 removed shared FAQ text, but the number of page-specific words did not grow ([C2](#critical), [H3](#high)).
3. **No authority, and the brand is ambiguous (High, both).** Linkar has 0 referring domains, against 19 for Zorcha and 593 for SuperProfile. It is missing from the directory pages that rank for "manychat alternative" ([H7](#high), [H12](#high)).
4. **A slow pricing page and a CSS regression in the build (High).** `/pricing` mobile LCP was 5.6 to 6.0 s in 7 of 7 runs, because the intro fades in from opacity 0. The build adds 18 to 21 KB gzip of render-blocking CSS ([H9](#high), [H10](#high)).
5. **The fixes are not live, and the deploy needs 24 untracked files (High, LIVE).** Leaving any of them out breaks the build or advertises sitemaps that 404 ([H1](#high), [H13](#high)).

**Top 5 quick wins**

1. In Cloudflare, turn on **Always Use HTTPS** (owner, 5 minutes). It also protects the `app.` and `admin.` sign-in pages, which load over plain HTTP today.
2. **Ship everything together.** `git add` the 24 untracked files, deploy, then request indexing for `/` and `/pricing` and remove the 2019 `www` sitemap from Search Console (owner).
3. **One config line and one keyframe.** Set `experimental.cssChunking: 'graph'` (G8) and make the pricing intro transform-only (G7).
4. **Give the head term one owner.** Point the `/solutions` hub's title and H1 at audience intent instead of "Instagram automation" (G9).
5. **Disambiguate the brand for AI answers.** Add a Facts section and a spelling line to llms.txt, and a description to the Organization schema (G10).

**Corrections found while verifying**

- **A sitemap is now submitted in Search Console.** `https://linkar.in/sitemap.xml` shows as submitted at 05:47 UTC (11:17 IST) on 15 Oct, with 12 URLs submitted and 0 indexed. A second entry, `https://www.linkar.in/sitemap.xml`, was submitted on 25 Sep 2019 and has 6 warnings. It predates Linkar and comes from a previous owner of the domain. Evidence: [2026-10-15/data/gsc-sitemaps.json](2026-10-15/data/gsc-sitemaps.json).
- **Linkar has four plans, not two.** Free ₹0, Creator ₹199, Growth ₹499 and Agency ₹999 a month, per [src/lib/billing/catalog.ts](../../src/lib/billing/catalog.ts) and live `/pricing`.
- **The CSP that scopes Google Analytics is not written in `next.config.ts`.** It lives in [src/lib/security/headers.ts](../../src/lib/security/headers.ts#L115-L122) and is generated from `ANALYTICS_PAGE_PATHS`. `next.config.ts` only calls it.
- **Live lab results vary a lot, so use medians.** Across today's pooled PSI runs (13 for home, 11 for pricing), home mobile LCP falls into two modes: 2.7 s, or 3.4 to 5.2 s. Pricing mobile LCP was 5.6 to 6.0 s in every run. Pricing TBT ranged from 68 to 670 ms, so the earlier single 670 ms reading is not a reliable baseline.
- **In Google India the head term is commercial.** Page 1 for "instagram automation" (gl=in, signed out, 15 Oct) has 4 vendor pages out of 9, with CreatorFlow's homepage first. It shares only 1 URL with "what is instagram automation". The strategy document gives the head term to a new pillar guide. This audit keeps it on the homepage and points the pillar at "what is instagram automation" ([data](2026-10-15/data/google-in-serps-2026-10-15.json), [seo-sxo SXO-01](2026-10-15/skill-reports/seo-sxo.md)).
- **Startup India (DPIIT) recognition is not open to a sole proprietorship,** which is Linkar's entity type ([legal-entity.ts:41](../../src/lib/legal-entity.ts#L41)). It is removed from the listing plan.
- **Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice now belong to G2.** The deal closed on 5 February 2026 (seo-backlinks). One Capterra listing plus a G2 profile covers all four.
- **The strategy's SERP snapshots pool two search engines.** For the same query the two share only 0 to 1 of 9 URLs, and one of the "4 shared URLs" in strategy section 4.3 is a spam host ([seo-cluster](2026-10-15/skill-reports/seo-cluster.md), section 7).
- **The competitor set is wider than the brief.** India AI answers cite ReplyKaro, FlowDM, QuickDM, UnlockDM, RapidDM, AutoDM, Repli and ViralDM, all priced in rupees. DM Linkr is a same-category name collision.
- **"Mark sign-up as a key event" cannot be done as written.** The site sends no sign-up event, only `page_view`. Section 7 gives the workaround.

## 3. Why the site has 0 impressions today

1. **Google has indexed nothing.**
   - URL Inspection for `https://linkar.in/` reads: "Crawled - currently not indexed". Last crawled 2026-09-24 22:41 UTC (mobile). The fetch succeeded, robots allowed it, indexing is allowed, and Google picked `https://linkar.in/` as canonical ([data/gsc-inspect-home.json](2026-10-15/data/gsc-inspect-home.json)).
   - `/pricing` and `/sitemap.xml` are "URL is unknown to Google" ([data/gsc-inspect-pricing.json](2026-10-15/data/gsc-inspect-pricing.json)).
   - Search performance shows 0 impressions and 0 clicks over 28, 90 and 480 days. A fresh query with data up to 15 Oct also returns 0 rows ([data/gsc-performance-480-days.json](2026-10-15/data/gsc-performance-480-days.json), [seo-google](2026-10-15/skill-reports/seo-google.md)).

   An unindexed site cannot get impressions.

2. **Google has not seen the current homepage over HTTPS, and it now prefers HTTP.**
   - The only HTTPS crawl of the homepage was on 24 Sep. The canonical tag, robots.txt, sitemap and metadata shipped on 10 Oct in commit `24e515c`. So Search Console's "no user-declared canonical" describes the older page, not a missing tag.
   - On 15 Oct at 07:37 UTC Googlebot crawled `http://linkar.in/`. The result: "Crawled - currently not indexed", Google canonical `http://linkar.in/`, user canonical `https://linkar.in/` ([data/gsc-inspect-http-home.json](2026-10-15/data/gsc-inspect-http-home.json)). Google treats the HTTP and HTTPS homepages as two separate URLs and ignores the declared canonical on the HTTP one.
   - Every HTTP URL I tried returns 200, with the same body as HTTPS: `/`, `/pricing`, `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `http://app.linkar.in/login` and `http://admin.linkar.in/login`. Only `http://www.` 301s.
   - The HTTP URL's referring pages are legacy links from the domain's earlier owners (rodrigoborges.info and rbmsoft.com.br). Those pages no longer resolve.

3. **The sitemap is only hours old.**
   - Google downloaded `/sitemap.xml` 2 seconds after it was submitted (05:47:32 UTC on 15 Oct), but has processed no URL through it yet.
   - Bing read it at 06:14 UTC (status Success, 12 URLs) but has not crawled the URLs. `/pricing` is undiscovered in Bing. Bing's homepage record dates from 2018 and holds a stored copy of only 1,033 bytes, against 309,587 bytes for the live HTML.

4. **No one links to Linkar.**
   - Bing Webmaster records 0 inbound links (15 Oct, 07:58 UTC).
   - linkar.in is not a vertex in the newest Common Crawl web graph (cc-main-2026-jul-aug-sep, 133 million domains). The Common Crawl URL index has no captures in CC-MAIN-2026-30, 34 or 39.
   - The backlink verification crawler found 0 links on 32 relevant pages.
   - GA4 shows 0 organic sessions and 0 AI-assistant referrals over 180 days.
   - For comparison, Common Crawl's sample counts these referring domains: ManyChat 10,771, SuperProfile 593 and Zorcha 19. These are lower bounds ([data/backlinks-commoncrawl-gap.json](2026-10-15/data/backlinks-commoncrawl-gap.json)).

   A new domain with no links gets crawled rarely and indexed cautiously.

5. **Other products own the brand name.**
   - A Google India search for "linkar" returns linkar.co.uk (a social rewards app in Manchester), three Play Store apps from other publishers, linkar.ink and linkar.cc.
   - The AI Overview says Linkar "most commonly refers to a social rewards mobile application".
   - A search for "linkar.in" returned a French vehicle-warranty company called Linkar and suggested the domain was a typo.
   - DM Linkr is an Instagram comment-to-DM tool in the same category.

   Until Google trusts the domain, a brand search does not even reach it.

6. **The domain has a history that Google has to re-evaluate (plausible factor, not proven).** The Wayback Machine has 439 captured URLs ([data/wayback-linkar-in-history.txt](2026-10-15/data/wayback-linkar-in-history.txt)):
   - **2015 to 2016:** a WordPress blog.
   - **2018 to 2019:** a Hindi Blogger blog on `www`, for example `/2018/12/chrome-browser-kaise-use-kare-janiye.html`. Its sitemap is still listed in Search Console.
   - **2022 to 2025:** a parked domain. Random short paths returned 302s to `ww16`, `ww25` and `ww38.linkar.in` with `sub1=` and `subid1=` tracking, plus `cpx.php`, `check_popunder.php` and `toolbar.php`.

   Today those subdomains do not resolve and the legacy paths return 404, which is correct. No toxic links are visible, so no disavow file is needed. Google still has to build trust in a repurposed domain from scratch.

7. **The page Google did crawl gives it little to rank.** The live homepage title is `Linkar · Instagram and Facebook automation`. The H1 is "Reply to every opportunity. Even when you are away." The visible text contains "Instagram automation" 0 times. 10 of the 12 sitemap URLs are legal or help pages.

   The better homepage (keyword title, H1 kicker, definition FAQ, linked schema) is in the local build. Part of it is in commits that are not pushed (`main` is 12 commits ahead of `origin/main`), and the G1 to G6 changes are not committed at all.

**What this means:** the first job is to get indexed, not to rank. The order is:

1. Turn on Always Use HTTPS, so Google stops treating the HTTP homepage as a page of its own.
2. Commit everything, including the 24 untracked files, and deploy.
3. Request indexing for `/` and `/pricing` over HTTPS, and submit the four sitemaps.
4. Earn the first real links and listings. Use one consistent description that ties "Linkar", "linkar.in" and "Instagram automation" together.
5. Release the catalogue at a quality level Google will accept.

Ranking for the head term "Instagram automation" against Manychat and the Indian alternatives will take months of links and content. Nothing here guarantees it.
